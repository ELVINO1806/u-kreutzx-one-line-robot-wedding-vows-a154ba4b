Competition: **One-Line Robot Wedding Vows** (`u-kreutzx-one-line-robot-wedding-vows-a154ba4b`) on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

This repository holds a one-line entry for the competition: the funniest wedding vow for a robot couple, written to sound spoken at a wedding, with a real promise at its heart and a machine-flavoured twist that lands in the final words.

The vow borrows the familiar rhythm of a traditional ceremony — "to have and to hold" — and rewires it with the everyday language of machines, so the joke feels like a genuine pledge rather than a gag pasted onto a wedding. The payoff sits at the very end of the line, where the classic closing phrase gets a hardware twist, which is exactly what makes it read well aloud in front of a room full of guests, human and robot alike.

## How it works

Running `main.py` writes the single entry line to `/output/entry.txt`.
